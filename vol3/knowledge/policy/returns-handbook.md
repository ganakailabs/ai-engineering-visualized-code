# Returns

## R1 Unused items
tags: product=ALL region=ALL doc_type=policy effective_from=2026-09-15
Unused items can be refunded within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## R2 Damaged items
tags: product=ALL region=ALL doc_type=policy effective_from=2026-09-15
Damaged items can be refunded or replaced within 7 days of delivery. The customer must send a photo of the damage with the request.

## R3 Final sale
tags: product=ALL region=ALL doc_type=policy effective_from=2026-09-15
Final-sale items cannot be refunded.

## S5 EU withdrawal
tags: product=ALL region=EU doc_type=policy effective_from=2026-09-15
Customers in the EU may withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days of delivery, without giving a reason.

## H1 Holiday returns
tags: product=ALL region=ALL doc_type=policy effective_from=2026-11-01
Items delivered from 1 November can be returned until 15 January.

## G1 Gift card note
tags: product=gift_card region=ALL
Gift cards can be exchanged for store credit.
