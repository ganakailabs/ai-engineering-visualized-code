=== page 1 ===
Attach the front grille by turning it clockwise until the clips lock.

Press the speed button to cycle through low, medium and high.
=== page 2 ===
To clean the blades, unplug the fan first, then remove the front grille.

Press and hold the swing button for 2 seconds to stop the fan turning from side to side.
