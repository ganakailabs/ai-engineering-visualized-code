=== page 1 ===
Thank you for choosing the NW-500. This guide covers charging, pairing and resetting.

Charge the headphones fully before first use. A full charge takes about 2 hours with the supplied cable.
=== page 2 ===
To pair, hold the power button for 3 seconds until the light flashes blue, then choose NW-500 on your phone.

To reset, hold the power button for 10 seconds until the light flashes red. Pair again after a reset.
=== page 3 ===
Warranty: NW-500 headphones carry a 12-month warranty against defects. A battery that stops charging counts as a defect.
