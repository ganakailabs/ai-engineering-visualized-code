=== page 1 ===
Screw the lamp head onto the arm until it clicks. Plug the cable into a wall socket.

Tap the base once to switch on. Tap again to change brightness. Hold the base for 2 seconds to switch off.
=== page 2 ===
Clean the lamp with a dry cloth only. Do not use water or sprays on the base.
